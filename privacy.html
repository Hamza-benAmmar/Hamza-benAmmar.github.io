Privacy Policy – SYW Pinterest Research App

Last updated: [9 November 2026]

This policy explains how Save your wardrobe Ltd. ("SYW", "we", "us") uses data obtained through the Pinterest API in our internal research application (the "App"). It applies only to this App. For our main services, see our general privacy policy: https://www.iubenda.com/privacy-policy/36628610.

2. What the App does

The App is an internal, experimental tool. It is not offered to the public and has no external users. It connects to Pinterest only through Pinterest's official API and OAuth sign-in, using SYW's own Pinterest account.
